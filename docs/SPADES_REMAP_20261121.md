## Full follow-up audit observations

The second user-supplied report (4,050 lines) is from the **same SHA-256 build** as the first. These are static metadata correlations; no runtime values were examined.

- `ceb.bfyw:Int32, bfyx:Int32, bfyy:UInt16, bfyz:Boolean` exactly match the old avatar progression field quartet.
- `ceb.bgbk:String, bgbl:String, bgbm:Byte, bgbn:Single, bgbo:String, bgbp:Int32` matches the previously documented `partyStatus, playerDbId, banditFlag, skinTonePercent, customAppearance, currency` sequence. **`bgbl` is therefore the strongest playerDbId candidate**. The values have not been read.
- `ceb.bgab:Dictionary<String,cxe>` and `ceb.bgac:cac` correspond to the earlier class-container and current-class member region. **Do not yet assume `cac` is PlayerClass** until its member signatures are checked.
- `cgv.bgma:ceb` + static `cgv.lwn():cgv` are stronger structural evidence for the player-character singleton than the earlier `ip` guess.
- `ip` has PowerUI.WorldUI, avatar, HtmlDocument and static self-return `dva()`; it is likely a **menu/controller**, not necessarily the character singleton.
- `cgw` has a compact class-like component layout with two Strings (`bgtk`, `bgtq`), a GameObject, multiple GameObjects and a `ceb` owner. Its exact gameplay role is unverified.

**Remaining offline gaps:** full `cac`, `cxe`, `cnw` metadata; source-function identity for `ceb` controller getter, vanilla rig refresh, and `cpc.ngd` submit. Existing preset writing must remain disabled until fully verified.
